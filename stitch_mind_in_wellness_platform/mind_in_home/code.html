Frequently Asked Questions

Is online therapy effective? expand_more

Yes, research consistently shows that online therapy is as effective as in-person therapy for most mental health concerns.

How long is each session? expand_more

Standard therapy sessions are typically 50 minutes long, often referred to as the 'therapeutic hour'.

What can I expect in the first session? expand_more

The first session is an intake where we discuss your history, current challenges, and what you hope to achieve through therapy.
